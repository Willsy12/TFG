
Nowadays, the Internet is a major source of information that helps us make decisions, often based on the opinions of other users. 
In the entertainment sector, ratings and reviews play a key role, influencing the choice of movies, music, or video games. 
As a result, multiple platforms have emerged that allow users to share their opinions and interact with others.

However, in the specific field of video games, there are few platforms that offer an experience focused on personal ratings, 
customized game lists, and social interaction between players. Therefore, this project proposes the development of a web application 
that enables users to rate video games, organize them into lists, and share their activity with other users, addressing a need that is still largely unmet in this sector.
